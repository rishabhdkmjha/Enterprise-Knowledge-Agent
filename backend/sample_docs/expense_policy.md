# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals
The domestic travel meal allowance is INR 1,500 per day. International travel allows USD 75 per day.

## Flights
Economy class is required for trips under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts. Claims above INR 25,000 need director approval.
