# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
All full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused days can be carried forward to the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.
